# Report

This folder contains the final thesis report for:

**Gen Z Adoption of AI in Banking: Evidence from Malaysia**

Original thesis title:

**Banking 4.0 in Malaysia: Is Gen Z Ready for It?**

The study investigates the factors influencing Malaysian Gen Z's intention to adopt AI in banking using an extended Technology Acceptance Model (TAM).

The research uses primary survey data from 633 respondents and applies Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modelling (PLS-SEM) to test the proposed relationships.

Key areas covered include:

- Perceived usefulness
- Perceived ease of use
- Attitude towards AI in banking
- Perceived risk
- Perceived trust
- Intention to adopt AI in banking
- Measurement-model validation
- Structural-model analysis
- Mediation effects
- Practical implications for banks and policymakers
